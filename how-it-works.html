How It Works

You set the rules. The program prepares tickets. You confirm each one. Every step is logged.

Try the live demoPractice data only. It never trades. The first load can take about a minute to wake up.

The member confirmed order flow

  1. You set the rules. Minimum gain, profit floor, fee buffer and daily limits are yours.
  2. The program finds lots. Semi-Auto finds lots that meet your rules. A buy scan finds funds that meet your buy criteria. Each match becomes a pending ticket: account, ticker, shares, limit price, which lot and the profit floor. A ticket is only a proposal. You can also create one by hand.
  3. You preview. The program reads a fresh quote and checks the lot, the profit floor, cash and reserve for buys, and the daily buy limits.
  4. You confirm. A box shows the order in words: side, shares, ticker, limit, account and estimated amount. You press Yes, place it.
  5. One limit order is sent. One click per order. There is no approve all.
  6. Everything is logged. Each refresh, scan, ticket, edit, preview, order, cancel and setting change goes in the Activity Log.

Safeguards on every order

Practice mode

The default. Tickets are logged as would place and nothing goes to a broker.

Kill switch

One click stops previews and orders. While it is on, nothing is sent.

No unattended trading

There is no automatic order placement. Every order needs your confirmation.

Lot must be named

A sell is blocked if the exact lot is unknown. The program never guesses a lot.

Profit floor

The limit is priced from a fresh quote and is never below your floor unless you explicitly confirm.

Duplicate and cap guards

Working orders are checked before a send. Daily buy order and dollar caps apply to every buy.

Data flow

Everything inside the dashed box happens on your own computer. Calls to your broker go straight from your computer to the broker. Nothing about your orders, accounts or positions passes through us.
