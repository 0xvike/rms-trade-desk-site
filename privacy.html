Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026.

The short version

RMS Trade Desk runs on your own computer. Your account data, positions, orders, broker keys and sign-in tokens stay on that computer. We do not receive them, we do not store them and we do not sell or share them.

This website

This website is static. It has no login, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising or tracking scripts. It loads nothing from outside sites. The site host (GitHub Pages) may keep ordinary server logs such as IP address, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

The program

  • Data on your computer. Rules, lots, tickets, the Activity Log and settings are saved in files on your computer. Broker keys and tokens are encrypted there. You can delete them at any time by removing the key in Settings or deleting the program's data folder.
  • Calls to your broker. The program sends requests straight from your computer to your broker (for example Schwab or E*TRADE) to read your accounts and, when you confirm, place orders. Your broker's own privacy policy governs that data.
  • Calls to data providers. The program may fetch fund and market data from third party data providers. These requests do not include your account numbers, balances or holdings.
  • The sign-in helper. If you choose Connect with Schwab, a small helper of ours handles the sign-in exchange and the 30 minute token refresh. It sees the tokens for the moment of the exchange, passes them back sealed so only your computer can open them, and keeps nothing. It does not log tokens and never sees orders, accounts or positions.
  • Problem reports. A report is built on your screen and sent from your own email program, so you see exactly what goes. It never includes keys, tokens or full account numbers. We receive it only if you send it.

Use of data

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or compile profiles. Information you email to support is used only to answer you.

Children

The program is for adults managing their own investment accounts.

Changes and contact

We will post any change on this page with a new date. Questions: support@cefcomplete.com (RMS Trade Desk support).
